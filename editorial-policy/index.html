DIRECTORY STANDARDS

Editorial policy.

Read the editorial principles governing listings, verification, corrections and removal requests on The gTLD Directory.

The gTLD Directory aims to provide a useful, factual and clearly presented overview of organisations serving the domain name industry. Listings are intended for discovery and research, not as recommendations or endorsements.

Listing information

Company names, descriptions, locations, websites and service categories may be based on information supplied by the organisation or gathered from publicly available sources. Information can change; visitors should confirm details directly with the organisation.

Verification

A listing marked “Not independently verified” has not undergone an independent verification process by the directory. A listing must not be interpreted as evidence of ICANN accreditation, contractual status, certification, technical capability or service quality.

Corrections and removal

Organisations and visitors may request corrections, updates or removal by emailing contact@gtld.directory. Please identify the listing and provide supporting information where relevant. Submission does not guarantee publication. The directory may edit, decline, update or remove listings to maintain relevance and clarity.
