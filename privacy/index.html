Privacy.

Privacy information for visitors and organisations using The gTLD Directory website.

This page describes, at a general level, how The gTLD Directory handles information submitted through or collected during use of this website.

Information you provide

If you contact the directory by email or use the company submission form, the information you choose to include may be used to respond to your enquiry and assess a proposed listing or update.

Website and technical information

Our hosting and security providers may process standard technical information, such as IP address, browser details and request logs, to deliver and protect the website. If analytics or advertising services are enabled, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own policies and applicable settings.

Sharing and retention

Information is not offered for sale. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the website or comply with legal obligations. Enquiry information is retained only as reasonably needed for its purpose.

Your choices

You may contact contact@gtld.directory to ask about information you have submitted or to request a correction or deletion, subject to applicable requirements.

This general notice should be reviewed to reflect the actual hosting, analytics, advertising and form-processing services in use.
