Registry service providers.

Organisations appearing in ICANN's 2026 Round Registry Service Provider (RSP) evaluation list, alongside other registry technology and operational providers.

This category brings together organisations identified in ICANN's published 2026 Round RSP list. The ICANN status shown on each profile is service-specific: successful evaluation for one service does not mean that every service has cleared evaluation. The ICANN list is updated as applicants progress. Directory inclusion is informational and does not constitute endorsement. Check ICANN's current list before making a provider selection.
